Troubleshooting

  1. Use your Cal Poly account
    Sign in with emplID@calpoly.edu — not a personal Gmail or Outlook account.
  2. Microsoft asks to pick an account?
    Choose your @calpoly.edu address from the list, or click "Use another account" and type it in.
  3. Getting "Access denied"?
    You signed in with the wrong account. Click "Try again" and use your Cal Poly email.
  4. Stuck in a loop?
    Clear your browser cookies for this site, then try again. In Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies → See all site data → search for this site → Delete.
  5. Still not working?
    Try opening in an incognito / private window to rule out cached credentials.
